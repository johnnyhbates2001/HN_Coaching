FAQs

Questions, answered

If you're new to coaching, it's natural to have questions. Here are some of the most common ones. If yours isn't here, just ask.

About coaching

What is life coaching?

Life coaching is a supportive, forward-focused conversation that helps you get clear on what you want, understand what's holding you back and take practical steps towards your goals. I won't give you the answers. I'll help you find your own.

How is coaching different from therapy or counselling?

Therapy often explores the past and helps people heal from difficult experiences or manage mental health conditions. Coaching focuses on the present and future: where you are now, where you want to be and how to get there. If I feel you'd benefit more from therapy, I'll always tell you honestly.

Who is coaching for?

Anyone who feels ready for a change and is willing to put in some effort to make it happen. You don't need to have everything figured out. Many people come to coaching precisely because they don't.

What results can I expect?

Most clients leave with more clarity, more confidence and a concrete plan. Real change comes from the actions you take between sessions, so the more you put in, the more you'll get out.

Sessions & booking

What happens on the free discovery call?

It's a relaxed 20-minute chat by phone or video. You can tell me a little about what's going on, ask any questions, and we'll both get a feel for whether we'd work well together. There's no obligation to book anything afterwards.

How long are sessions and how often do we meet?

Sessions last 60 minutes. Most clients meet every two to three weeks, which gives time to take action between sessions, but we can agree whatever works best for you.

Do you offer sessions online?

Yes. Sessions can take place by video call, by phone, or in person. Online sessions work just as well as face-to-face for most people.

How do I pay?

Payment is by bank transfer before each session, or before the first session for programmes. I'll send you all the details once you've decided to go ahead.

What if I need to cancel or rearrange?

Life happens! Just let me know at least 48 hours in advance and we'll rearrange at no cost. Sessions cancelled with less notice may be charged.

Privacy

Is what I share confidential?

Absolutely. Everything you share stays between us. The only exception would be if I believed you or someone else was at serious risk of harm. See the privacy policy for how your personal details are handled.

Still have a question?

I'm always happy to help. Send me a message and I'll get back to you within two working days.
