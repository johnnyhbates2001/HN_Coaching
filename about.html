My story

Why I became a coach

For many years I was the person friends, family and colleagues came to when they needed someone to listen. I noticed that people rarely need to be told what to do. What they need is space to think, the right questions, and someone in their corner.

After my own experience of big life changes, I trained as a life coach so I could offer that support properly. I work with people at all stages of life who feel ready for something to shift.

I bring life experience, genuine curiosity and plenty of warmth to every session. I'm not here to judge. I'm here to help you move forward.
